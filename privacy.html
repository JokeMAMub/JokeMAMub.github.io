Privacy Policy

Application: Personal Drive Sync

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Purpose and Google Drive access

This application is a personal desktop workflow for uploading the owner's selected files and folders from a local Windows computer to the owner's own Google Drive via rclone. The application uses Google OAuth only with the owner's permission and only to support the configured file-transfer workflow.

Information accessed and used

Depending on the Google Drive permissions granted to rclone, it may access Drive file names, identifiers, folder structure, file contents, and related metadata necessary for uploads, file checks, and other actions the owner initiates. The owner controls the account, local source files, destination folders, and configuration.

Storage and transfers

OAuth tokens and rclone configuration are stored locally on the owner's computer. Files are transferred between the owner's computer and Google's Drive service over encrypted connections. This public website is informational and does not itself receive the owner's Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Sharing and sale

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or intentionally disclose it to unrelated third parties. Google processes data as needed to provide Google Drive. The user controls any separate sharing settings on their own Drive.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The rclone configuration, authentication tokens, and optional local transfer logs remain on the owner's computer until removed by the owner. The owner may revoke app access in their Google Account settings at any time.

Security

Credentials are not published on this website. The owner is responsible for protecting the local computer and configuration files. This website includes no login form, tracking script, or file upload mechanism.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's functions or data handling practices change.
